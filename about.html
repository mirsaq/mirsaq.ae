Who We Are

Company Overview

MIRSAQ General Trading LLC is a licensed trading company registered in the United Arab Emirates and headquartered in Dubai. We operate as a B2B electronics wholesale exporter, sourcing genuine factory-sealed consumer electronics and supplying them in bulk quantities to importers, distributors, retailers and resellers worldwide.

Dubai's position as a global re-export hub gives MIRSAQ a natural advantage: direct access to authorized distributor stock, competitive pricing, and fast air freight connections to every major market. We do not sell to end consumers — our business is purely wholesale (B2B), with minimum order quantities starting from 3–10 units depending on the product category.

Our core markets are CIS countries (Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Belarus, Ukraine, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Georgia), Sub-Saharan Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya), the Maldives, and the broader Middle East and Europe. We have active wholesale clients in over 50 countries.

How We Work

Our Process

Step 1 — Inquiry. Contact us via WhatsApp or email with the products and quantities you need.

Step 2 — Proforma Invoice. We send a proforma invoice with unit prices, shipping cost, and total. Typically within 2 hours.

Step 3 — Payment. Payment by bank wire transfer (USD/AED/EUR), Western Union, or USDT. New clients pay 100% upfront.

Step 4 — Shipping. Orders shipped within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation. Airway bill and tracking number shared immediately.

Step 5 — Delivery. Express air freight. Typically 3–7 business days to most destinations worldwide.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MIRSAQ General Trading LLC?

MIRSAQ General Trading LLC is a licensed UAE trading company based in Dubai. We are a B2B electronics wholesale exporter supplying genuine, factory-sealed consumer electronics — iPhones, Samsung, Garmin, Dyson, JBL, PlayStation and more — to bulk buyers worldwide.

Is MIRSAQ a licensed company in the UAE?

Yes. MIRSAQ General Trading LLC is a licensed trading company registered in the United Arab Emirates. We operate under a UAE general trading license.

Does MIRSAQ sell to individuals or only businesses?

MIRSAQ is a wholesale-only (B2B) supplier. We sell in bulk quantities to importers, distributors, retailers, and resellers — not to individual end consumers.

Are the products genuine and under warranty?

Yes. All products supplied by MIRSAQ are 100% genuine, factory-sealed, and carry the manufacturer's global or regional warranty. We do not deal in refurbished, grey-market, or counterfeit goods.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product: smartphones and smartwatches start from 3 units, audio accessories from 10 units, and smaller accessories from 50 units per SKU. Contact us to confirm MOQ for your specific product.

How long has MIRSAQ been in business?

MIRSAQ General Trading LLC was founded in 2022 and has since grown to serve wholesale clients in over 50 countries, with particular strength in CIS markets and Africa.
