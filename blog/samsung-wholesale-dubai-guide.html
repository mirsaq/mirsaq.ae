How to Verify Samsung Authenticity on Arrival

When your Samsung wholesale order arrives, carry out these checks before accepting the shipment:

  • Confirm factory seals are intact on every unit
  • Check each device's IMEI by dialing *#06# — the IMEI displayed must match your commercial invoice
  • Verify the IMEI on Samsung's official website or through the Samsung Members app
  • Power on each device and confirm it boots correctly without any pre-existing Samsung account lock
  • Cross-check the model number on the device settings with what you ordered

Any discrepancy between the physical IMEI and your invoice should be raised with your freight carrier immediately before signing for the shipment.
