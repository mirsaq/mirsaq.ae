How to Verify iPhone Authenticity from Dubai

Every genuine iPhone from MIRSAQ can be verified in multiple ways before and after purchase:

  • Apple Coverage Check — visit checkcoverage.apple.com and enter the IMEI. A genuine device shows warranty status, model, and purchase eligibility.
  • Box seal — genuine Apple packaging has a transparent seal with Apple logos. Never accept a device with a broken or replaced seal.
  • Settings → General → About — model number, IMEI, and serial number should all be consistent and match the box.
  • FaceTime and iMessage activation — genuine unlocked iPhones activate these services immediately with any SIM card.

MIRSAQ provides IMEI verification for all iPhones before dispatch. Buyers can request IMEI numbers in advance for spot-checking on large orders.
