Shipping Electronics from Dubai to Russia

Direct Air Freight Dubai – Moscow

The fastest route. Direct air freight from Dubai (DXB) to Moscow Sheremetyevo (SVO) or Domodedovo (DME) operates daily with multiple carriers. Transit time is typically 2–4 days for cargo. MIRSAQ prepares air waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin for Russian customs.

Transit via Armenia

Dubai to Yerevan (EVN) by air, then road freight Yerevan–Moscow via the Russian border. Popular route for consolidating multiple shipments. Total transit 5–10 days. Armenia is an EAEU member, simplifying customs procedures at the Russian border.

Transit via Kazakhstan

Dubai to Almaty (ALA) by air, then road or rail freight to Moscow. Kazakhstan is also an EAEU member. Often used for larger shipments and sea freight containers. Transit 7–15 days.

Transit via Georgia

Dubai to Tbilisi (TBS) by air, then road freight through Russia (Verkhny Lars border crossing). Suitable for smaller consolidated shipments. Transit 5–8 days.

Russian Customs Documentation for Electronics
