Step 3: Payment Terms and Documentation

Standard B2B payment terms for iPhone wholesale from Dubai in 2026:

  • Wire transfer (T/T) — the most common method for international buyers
  • Typical terms: 30–50% deposit, balance paid before shipment
  • Documents provided: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin (COO), IMEI list
  • Currency: USD is standard; some suppliers accept EUR or AED

Always ensure you receive a signed commercial invoice listing each IMEI number before your shipment departs. This is essential for customs clearance in most countries.

Step 4: Shipping Options from Dubai

iPhones are high-value, low-weight goods — air freight is almost always used. Your main options from Dubai:

  • Express courier (DHL, FedEx, Aramex): 3–5 days door-to-door, easiest for customs, best for smaller quantities
  • Air cargo (Emirates SkyCargo, Etihad Cargo): lower cost per kg for large quantities, 2–4 days flight time, requires a customs broker at destination

Step 6: Verify Authenticity on Arrival

When your order arrives, verify each unit before accepting the shipment:

  • Check that factory seals are intact on every unit
  • Verify each IMEI on Apple's website at checkcoverage.apple.com
  • Confirm model and storage match your commercial invoice
  • Power on each device and confirm it is not iCloud / activation locked
  • Cross-check IMEI list on invoice against physical units
